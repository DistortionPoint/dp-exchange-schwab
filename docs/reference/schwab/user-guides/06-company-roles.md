What are Company Roles and how are they used?

Companies are entities that allow a group of registered users to function and act on behalf of a legal entity. All members of a Company are bound to legal agreements and terms of use for any Products, APIs, Apps and documentation accessed. Within a Company, controls are provided to allow distinction between Administrator and Developer roles for Companies.

The Registered User that creates the Company Profile is automatically assigned role of Company Admin.

Company Admin may perform the following functions:

May request access to Products grouped by Line of Business or functional grouping.
May accept and approve legal agreements with each Line of Business to get API and Documentation access.
May modify/update the Company Profile.
May invite other Developers to the Company. Note: The Company must be granted access to at least one Line of Business' Product set to unlock the invite member functionality.
May promote, demote, and remove Developers from the Company.
Company Admin may request Company and Company Apps to be promoted to the Production (Live data) environment.
Is required to perform periodic attestation of User access for members of their Company.

Company Developers are limited to the following functions, which the Company Admin may also perform:

May create and manage Company Apps.
May read all Product documentation, guides and support docs when granted access by a Line of Business.
May perform “Try Now” functions for API Products accessible to the Company.
May request Support on behalf of a Company.
Note:

Company Admins do not have the ability to remove themselves from the company or company admin role.
If you wish to be removed from the company, or demote your role to a company developer, you must first promote another company member to be a company admin. Once promoted, that user will be able to demote your company role. This is to ensure that your company will always have at least one company admin.
