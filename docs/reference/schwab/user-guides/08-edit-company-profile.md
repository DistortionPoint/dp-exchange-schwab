How do I edit my company's profile?

To edit your company’s profile in Dev Portal:

On your Company Dashboard Page, click the Company Profile link next to your company name.
This will redirect you to your Company Profile page. To edit the information, click the Edit button at the bottom of the page.
Make any necessary changes to the Company Profile and click Save.
Note: Company name is locked. If you need to edit your company name, please submit a support request to have the development team rename your company.
