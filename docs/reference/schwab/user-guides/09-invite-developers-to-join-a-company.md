# Invite Developers to Join a Company

Captured: 2026-08-31 from the signed-in Schwab Developer Portal.

---

How do I invite other developers to join my Company?

Note:

Only Company Administrators may invite users to join a company.
Your Company must be approved for at least one API Product before you can invite users to join.

Company/Role Selector

The Developer Portal allows users to have multiple roles or be members of multiple companies. If your account has multiple roles or company memberships, you must first select your company from the “Role Selector” dropdown located in the main menu.
If you do not have multiple roles or company membership, this selection will not appear.

To invite developers to join your company:

Click the Dashboard Link in the Main Menu.
At the bottom of this page, use the Invite User field to enter the email address of the user and submit by clicking Invite.
User will receive an email with a link to register for the Developer Portal (if this step is not yet completed) or they will receive an email with a link to view / accept your invitation to join your Company
Note: Unaccepted invitations will expire in 7 Days. You can re-send an expired invitation from the Members Table or Resubmit the invite user form.
