# Create Company Profile

Source: https://developer.schwab.com/user-guides/third-party-company/create-company-profile
Captured: 2026-08-31 from the signed-in Schwab Developer Portal.

---

Company Profiles allow Schwab to properly identify and validate a business, organization, or legal entity before granting API Product access to protected resources. The Company Profile also allows API Product Administrators to understand development needs for Apps you will create and reach out if there are any questions. Once a Company Profile is complete, your Company can request API Product access.

Note:

A Registered User that creates a Company Profile automatically becomes the Company Profile Owner with a Company Admin role. See Company Roles knowledge base article for additional information.
If your Company already exists in the Dev Portal, contact your Company Admin to send you an invitation to join that Company. Users are unable to join an existing Company without an invitation from the Company Admin.
Check with your Company's business or development team leads to prevent duplicate creation of Company Profiles. Your Company may already have API Product access with active Apps under an existing Company Profile within the Dev Portal

To create a company profile:

Create Company Profile from the Welcome Page
Upon successful User Registration, the first time you login to the Dev Portal, you will be redirected to the Welcome Page.
On the New Company card, click the Create button to be taken directly to a form to create your company profile.

From the menu
You may also create a Company at any time through the secondary menu that appears once you have successfully authenticated on the Dev Portal.
Select Create Company from the menu to be taken directly to a form to create your company profile.

Company Profile Information

Several fields of information are required (unless noted as optional). Fields include, but are not limited to: Company Name, Business Address; Company Website, and App Use Cases. Upon submission of this information, an onscreen message will confirm completion.

Once your Company registration is complete, you will be granted the role of Company Admin which allows you to request access to available API Products, Invite developers, and manage company member roles.

Note:

Your Company must have at least one API Product authorized for access, before you can invite developers to join your Company.
